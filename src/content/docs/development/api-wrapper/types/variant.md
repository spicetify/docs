---
title: Variant
description: Spotify's font variant names.
---

`Variant` names a type style in Spotify's Encore theme. `TextComponent` takes one in its `variant` prop.

```ts
type Variant =
  | 'bass'
  | 'forte'
  | 'brio'
  | 'altoBrio'
  | 'alto'
  | 'canon'
  | 'celloCanon'
  | 'cello'
  | 'ballad'
  | 'balladBold'
  | 'viola'
  | 'violaBold'
  | 'mesto'
  | 'mestoBold'
  | 'metronome'
  | 'finale'
  | 'finaleBold'
  | 'minuet'
  | 'minuetBold';
```
